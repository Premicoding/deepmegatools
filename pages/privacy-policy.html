Deep Mega Tools provides online utilities for developers, creators, and general users. Some tools may process information that you enter or files that you upload.

We do not intentionally request personal information unless it is necessary for a particular service. Technical information, such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and usage data, may be collected by website hosting, analytics, or other services used on this website.

Information may be used to:

  • Understand general website usage.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Respond to messages sent to us.

3. Uploaded Files and Tool Inputs

Some tools allow you to enter text or upload files for processing. Where a tool runs entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript, the text or file you provide is processed on your own device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Where a tool requires server-side processing, the content you submit is transmitted to our server only for the purpose of performing the requested operation and is not retained beyond what is necessary to complete that operation, unless the tool states otherwise.

Please do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information to any tool unless you have verified how that specific tool processes your data.

4. Cookies

Our website or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, measure usage, remember preferences, or provide advertising.

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features. Where required by applicable law, non-essential cookies are set only after you have given consent through the consent mechanism shown on this website.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) or similar technologies to show, measure, or personalize advertisements based on a user's prior visits to this or other websites.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

If personalized advertising or cookies require your consent under applicable laws, we will only set such cookies after you have given consent through the consent mechanism shown on this website.
