What is an Age Calculator?

An Age Calculator is an online tool that computes the difference between your Date of Birth (DOB) and the current date to determine your exact age.

For example, if you were born on January 15, 2000, the Age Calculator will show your current age in years, months, and days, along with the total time lived in weeks, hours, minutes, and seconds.

It's useful for filling out forms, verifying age requirements, or simply satisfying your curiosity about how old you really are.
