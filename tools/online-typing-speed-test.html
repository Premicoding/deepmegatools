What Is a Typing Speed Test?

A typing speed test is an online tool that measures how quickly and accurately you can type text using a keyboard. Typing speed is generally measured in Words Per Minute (WPM). It helps you understand your typing skills and identify areas where you need improvement.

For example, if you can type 40 words in one minute, your typing speed is approximately 40 WPM. A typing test also measures accuracy, which indicates how many characters you type correctly compared to the total number of characters entered.

🧮 How Is Typing Speed Calculated?

WPM = Correct Characters ÷ 5 ÷ Time in Minutes

Example: Suppose you type 250 correct characters in one minute.
WPM = 250 ÷ 5 ÷ 1 = 50 WPM

Accuracy = Correct Characters ÷ Total Typed Characters × 100

If you type 200 characters and 190 are correct:
Accuracy = (190 ÷ 200) × 100 = 95%

⚡ 10 Tips to Improve Typing Speed & Accuracy

1
Practice every day — 10–15 minutes regularly.
2
Learn proper finger placement — home row: A S D F J K L ;
3
Focus on accuracy first — reduce mistakes before speed.
4
Avoid looking at the keyboard — practice touch typing.
5
Maintain proper posture — sit straight, hands correctly positioned.
6
Practice with different texts — articles, code, sentences.
7
Set daily typing goals — improve by 2–5 WPM.
8
Use all your fingers — correct fingering improves efficiency.
9
Track your progress — compare previous scores.
10
Stay consistent — short daily practice beats occasional marathons.
