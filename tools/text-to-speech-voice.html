What Is a Text to Speech Tool?

A Text to Speech (TTS) tool converts written text into spoken audio using speech synthesis technology. Instead of reading a long paragraph manually, you type or paste it into the tool and let your device read it aloud.

This tool uses your browser's built-in Web Speech API (SpeechSynthesis) — which means the audio is generated locally on your device and is not uploaded to any server.

💡 Did you know? Modern browsers like Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox support speech synthesis natively. The available voices depend on your operating system and installed language packs.

Limitations & Important Tips

  • Voice availability varies — depends on OS, browser & installed language packs.
  • Gender detection is approximate — based on voice name keywords.
  • Long text may pause — some browsers (like Chrome) auto-cut speech after ~15 seconds. Use Pause → Resume as a workaround.
  • Mobile browsers — iOS Safari limits voice selection; Android Chrome works best.
  • Offline — works only if voices are installed locally.
  • No MP3 download — browsers don't allow saving synthesized speech directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Text to Speech tool really free?

Yes. It uses your browser's built-in speech engine — no subscription, no account, no hidden charges.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Just open the page in a supported browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) and start using it.

Can I choose a male or female voice?

Yes. Use the All / Male / Female filter. If no matching voice is found, the tool automatically falls back to all available voices.

Why do I see different voices than my friend?

Voices are provided by your operating system and browser. Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS each have different voice sets.

Can I download the audio as MP3?

Not directly. Browsers restrict saving synthesized speech. For MP3 export you'd need a server-side TTS service.

Does it work offline?

Partially. If your device has offline voices installed, yes. Otherwise an internet connection may be needed.

Is my text private?

Yes — speech is generated locally by your browser. Avoid pasting sensitive data into any online tool as a general safety rule.
